gronka

terms

gronka on the web is free, for everyone, with the same limits for everyone. a few rules keep it that way.

what it's for

  • saving things for your own use: a clip to keep, a video to watch offline, a sound for an edit.
  • it's your job to have the right to download and use what you save. respect the people who made it and the sites' rules. don't re-upload other people's work as your own.
  • gronka only fetches what a site already shows publicly or what gronka itself can see. it doesn't break into private accounts.

don't

  • hammer it. there are limits (about 10 downloads per 10 minutes, one at a time, a 400 mb cap on files we fetch ourselves) and trying to get around them, with many addresses or many keys, gets you blocked.
  • use it to download anything illegal, or to harass anyone.
  • point it at things that aren't media links: internal addresses, scanners, other people's servers.
  • resell it or put it behind your own paywall.

api keys

keys are for scripts, apps and agents. they skip the cloudflare check and get the same limits as everyone else. keep them secret; a key that is abused gets revoked.

no promises

this is a public preview run by one person on one machine. it may be slow, down, or changed without notice, and sites break downloaders all the time. gronka comes with no warranty of any kind, and isn't liable for what you do with what you download.

takedowns

gronka keeps no files beyond an hour, so there's usually nothing to take down. if you own something and want gronka to stop fetching it, say so on gronka.dev.
