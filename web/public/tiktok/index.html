what works

  • video links from the app or the site, including vm.tiktok.com share links.
  • pick audio for an mp3 of the sound, or no sound for the video on its own.
  • trim cuts a part out of a longer video.

how the file gets to you

gronka asks for the video one way and, if that fails, tries a second way. the second way is also the one that can reach age-restricted posts. more on that.

questions

why do some tiktoks take longer?

when the first way fails gronka tries the second, and that adds a few seconds.

do i need an account?

no. paste the link and download. accounts only exist to hold api keys.

is there a limit?

about 10 downloads per 10 minutes, one at a time. the same for everyone, and nothing is paid.
