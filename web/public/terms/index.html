terms

gronka on the web is free, for everyone, with the same limits for everyone. a few rules help keep it that way.

what it's for

  • save things for yourself: a clip to keep, a video to watch offline, a sound for an edit.
  • make sure you have the right to download and use what you save. respect the people who made it and the sites' rules. don't re-upload other people's work as your own.
  • gronka only fetches what a site already shows publicly or what gronka itself can see. it doesn't break into private accounts.

don't

  • don't hammer it. limits are about 50 downloads per 10 minutes, and 400 mb for files we fetch ourselves. using many addresses or keys to get around them gets you blocked.
  • use it to download anything illegal, or to harass anyone.
  • point it at things that aren't media links: internal addresses, scanners, other people's servers.
  • resell it or put it behind your own paywall.

api keys

keys are for scripts, apps and agents. they skip the cloudflare check and get the same limits as everyone else. keep them secret; a key that is abused gets revoked.

no promises

this public preview runs on one machine, maintained by one person. it may be slow, go down, or change without notice. sites break downloaders all the time. gronka comes with no warranty of any kind and isn't liable for how you use your downloads.

takedowns

gronka keeps no files beyond an hour, so there's usually nothing to take down. if you own something and want gronka to stop fetching it, say so on gronka.dev.
